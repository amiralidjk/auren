Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · See also our Terms of Sale and Refunds.

Who we are

Auren is a Windows desktop app made by an independent developer. Questions about this policy can be sent through our support page.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID. We use them only to recognise you and remember that you've paid.
  • Payment status. We store whether you've bought Auren, when, and the Stripe checkout reference. We never see or store your card details: payments are handled entirely by Stripe.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, layouts and listening statistics are stored only on your PC. Auren reads the music playing on your computer to drive its visuals; this is not uploaded anywhere.

Online services Auren uses

  • Supabase stores your account and payment status (servers in the EU).
  • Google handles sign-in.
  • Stripe handles payment.
  • LRCLIB (lyrics), iTunes / Deezer (album art) and Open-Meteo (weather) receive the song or location details needed to answer each request. Auren does not send them your account details.
  • GitHub is checked for new versions when you look for updates.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your information for marketing.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and payment status for as long as you use Auren, so you can reinstall without paying again. To have your account deleted, contact us through the support page; we'll delete it within 30 days. Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used. Contact us through the support page. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
